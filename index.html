The problem

Thousands of houses. One report each. Written days later, from memory.

The visit takes a morning. The write-up takes the week, at a desk, from a camera roll and a notebook. Then the lab, the council and the homeowner each get their own copy, and each asks for something that is missing.

4people per case
Assessor, lab, council, homeowner. Today, four versions of the truth.
One case, many hands

Everyone sees the same case. Nobody re-types it.

ASSESSOR

Writes it at the house.

Photos, voice notes and old paperwork become the draft. They correct it and sign it.

LAB

Adds the results.

Core samples and test sheets land in the same case, not in an email thread.

COUNCIL

Gets the same PDF it gets today.

Frozen, dated, and the same file next year. No new portal to learn.

HOMEOWNER

Sees where their house stands.

A one-time link to upload what is missing and read the version they were sent. Nothing else.

Nobody re-types. Nobody chases. Nobody gets a login they did not ask for.

How it works

Prepare. Capture. Correct. Freeze.

01

Prepare from what exists.

Upload the planning file and any old report. The blank fields fill in, each one showing which document it came from.

02

Capture at the house.

Walk the walls and rooms. Photos and voice notes save on the tablet first and upload when there is signal.

03

Correct the draft.

The report is already written. Fix what is wrong. Anything you type is never overwritten by the machine.

04

Freeze and share.

Version 1 becomes a PDF that never changes. Send it to the council, the lab or the homeowner. Edit again and you get version 2.

The AI

The model proposes. The assessor signs.

IT PROPOSES

Every suggestion shows its source.

"Year built: 2006, from planning.pdf." "Vertical crack near the corner, from your voice note." Nothing appears without a where-from.

YOU DECIDE

It never overwrites a person.

A blank field takes the suggestion. A field you typed keeps your value. Run it again as often as you like.

YOU SIGN

Nothing is signed by a machine.

The declaration is empty until the assessor fills it. The PDF says "draft" until they say otherwise.

In this prototype every suggestion comes from a labelled stand-in, not a live model. The live connection exists and has not been tested on real documents.
