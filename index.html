Releve

Your defective block assessments,
drafted before you leave the site.

Every application to the Defective Concrete Blocks Grant Scheme needs a Building Condition Assessment. The visit takes a morning; writing it up can take two weeks. Releve's AI reads the homeowner's paperwork, your site photos and your voice notes, and drafts the report on site.

The scale

This is not a Donegal problem.

20,000 Homes may be affected

Petrolab's survey of 48 estates, presented to the Oireachtas in June 2026. It excludes social housing, businesses and duplexes. Councils have counted 9,500 so far.

9 Counties in the scheme

6 in full, parts of 3Carlow, Clare, Donegal, Limerick, Mayo and Sligo, plus parts of Fingal, Meath and Wexford.

8,000 Of them outside Donegal

Petrolab projects 12,000 in Donegal, 4,500 in Clare, 2,500 in Limerick and 1,000 in Mayo. Two in five are elsewhere.

Petrolab analysis presented at Leinster House, June 2026 (RTÉ News) · CCMA to the Oireachtas Housing Committee, 2026 · Dept. of Housing scheme statistics to 30 June 2026

The bottleneck

Every application to the scheme starts with this report.

A registered engineer, architect or surveyor must inspect the house and sign a Building Condition Assessment before the homeowner can apply.

3,598

Applications so far.

Every one of them needed a signed assessment before it could be made, and thousands of homes have yet to apply.

Dept. of Housing, to 30 June 2026
Scattered

Evidence arrives in pieces.

Phone photos, voice memos, handwritten notes, planning files and old reports, retyped at a desk days later.

The difference

Not a black box. Every line traces back.

Paste documents into a chatbot and you still have to check every answer. Releve breaks each source into small facts, and every line of the report points to the fact it came from.

  • Reads PDFs
  • Transcribes voice notes
  • Pulls out facts with page references
  • Drafts the narrative
  • Answers questions with citations

How it scales

Evidence in, a source for every value, a signature, a version that never changes. That engine fits any report a professional has to sign, so a new report type is a new template, not a new product.
